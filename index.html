First, are you registered to vote at your school address?

Michigan students can register at their campus address. If you are not registered yet, you still have time, and you can register in person right through Election Day.

Let us get you registered first.

Registering at your campus address takes a few minutes. Once you are on the roll, come back here and we will show you where to vote.

Register with the State of Michigan Check my status
In Michigan you can register and vote on the same day, including Election Day, at your city or township clerk's office. Bring proof of where you live, such as a housing contract, a utility bill, or a student ID with a current address document.
